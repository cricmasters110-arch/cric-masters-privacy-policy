📄 Privacy Policy – Cric Masters
Last updated: 23-Jan-2026
Cric Masters (“we”, “our”, “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use the Cric Masters mobile application (“App”).
1. Information We Collect
We may collect the following types of information:
a) Information You Provide
Team names, player names, match details entered for scoring purposes
b) Automatically Collected Information
Device information (device model, OS version)
App usage data (screens visited, features used)
Crash logs and performance data
We do not collect personally identifiable information such as phone numbers, addresses, or payment details unless explicitly stated.
2. How We Use Information
The collected information is used to:
Enable cricket scoring and match management
Generate scorecards and match summaries (including PDFs)
Improve app performance and user experience
Fix bugs and crashes
3. Data Sharing
We do not sell or share user data with third parties, except:
Analytics or crash-reporting services (if enabled) for app improvement
When required by law or legal process
4. Data Storage & Security
Match data is stored locally on your device unless otherwise stated
We take reasonable measures to protect data from unauthorized access
5. Children’s Privacy
Cric Masters is intended for users 13 years and above.
We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.
6. Permissions Used
The app may request permissions such as:
Storage – to export and save scorecards/PDFs
Internet – for analytics or future cloud features
Permissions are used strictly for app functionality.
7. Your Rights
You can:
Clear app data from your device
Uninstall the app to stop data collection

Any changes will be reflected on this page.
